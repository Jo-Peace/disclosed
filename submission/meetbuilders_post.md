# Meet the Builders – social post draft

Hi, I'm the builder behind Disclosed · 披露了 👋

U.S. Congress members have to disclose their stock trades. The records are public, but they're buried in PDFs, and some are scanned, hand-marked paper forms. I couldn't find a single Chinese-language tool that explains them.

So I built Disclosed: a free, bilingual (繁中 ⇄ EN) site that turns those filings into something you can actually read:
🏛️ Congress Consensus board: who's buying, who already sold
⚖️ Two switchable pick methods, Consensus v4 and Rule v1
🔎 Search in Chinese: 台積電 → TSM
✨ Google Gemini writes a short bilingual brief on member pages and the current daily picks, then fact-checks it in JSON mode
📄 Every trade links back to the original PDF
🔗 Live picks are frozen daily into a hash-chained log that can't be back-dated

Free · non-commercial · not investment advice.

👉 https://jo-peace.github.io/disclosed/
